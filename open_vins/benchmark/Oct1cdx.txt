1 OCTOBER 2026

HW290 OpenVINS drift investigation

Introduction

I am testing OpenVINS and ORB-SLAM3 with my HW290 IMU and stereo camera for
reliable motion tracking and lower computational cost. After securing the
wiring, OpenVINS still produced false meter- and kilometer-scale trajectories.
Today I focused on calibration and checking movement within my desk limits.

Work done

I restored camera texture with 5 ms exposure and gain 255, but my first desk
test still reached 7.46 m. I completed the screen calibration capture, checked
40 mm tag edges and 12 mm gaps, and fitted the stereo cameras and IMU alignment.
I fixed the capture countdown and IMU recording queue, added estimator input
counters, and made image rectification and static TF follow the selected profile.
I also updated the overview and comprehensive pipeline model.

Results

The fitted stereo baseline is 5.945 cm. Camera residual spread is about
0.26-0.32 pixels; IMU alignment changed about 26.5 degrees and timing is 13.04 ms.
On the same recorded movement, the old model reached 18.92 km and the new model
stayed below 0.47 m. Comparison against the camera-derived board reference gave
2.67 cm RMSE after the fitting interval; this is not independent ground truth.

My fresh two-minute continuous desk test stayed within the limits with no
flights. Across 868 seconds of saved poses, maximum displacement was 0.852 m,
below the 1.72 m desk diagonal.
The estimator averaged 14.82 ms per tracking/update cycle, 56.5% of one CPU core,
and 185.1 MiB peak memory. Recording and RViz costs are reported separately.

Conclusion and future work

The new calibration removed the large flights in replay and my physical test,
so I made it the OpenVINS default. Independent ground truth is still needed
to measure trajectory accuracy.

IMU noise needs long stationary measurement; the fitted 6.7 cm lever arm needs
a better physical check. The bag had no IMU gaps above 25 ms, but OpenVINS
logged 46 input gaps above 50 ms. I still need to check estimator delivery,
update ORB calibration, and run a matched compute and accuracy comparison.
