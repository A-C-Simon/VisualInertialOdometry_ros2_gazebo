HW290 OpenVINS drift investigation

I analyzed the latest 39.90 m failure using its complete diagnostic bag. The
IMU stayed clean, but the fixed 5 ms camera exposure made many frames nearly
black. Around the first trajectory departure, about 98 percent of pixels were
below intensity 10 and the two stereo images had as few as 1 and 10 ORB
keypoints. Visual correction was therefore lost before the false flight.

I tested the camera controls directly and found that usb_cam 0.8.1 kept the
device in UVC manual exposure mode even when `autoexposure` was enabled in its
YAML file. I updated the launcher to force UVC automatic exposure mode 3 after
camera startup, with brightness and backlight compensation set to zero. A clean
restart confirmed the requested mode. The launcher now reads the controls back
and stops if the camera rejected automatic mode. Sampled ROS images reached the
1,500 ORB keypoint test cap in each stereo half and had negligible clipping.

I also replayed the earlier failure with a 10 ms camera-to-IMU offset. It
worsened the maximum displacement to 16.32 m, compared with 0.958 m using the
calibrated 20 ms offset and the current innovation gate. I restored and kept
the 20 ms calibration. A two-minute continuous desk-motion test is still needed
to confirm that the exposure correction prevents false meter-scale flight.
