1 OCTOBER 2026

HW290 OpenVINS drift investigation

I analyzed the latest 39.90 m failure using its complete diagnostic bag. The
IMU stayed clean, but the fixed 5 ms camera exposure made many frames nearly
black. Around the first trajectory departure, about 98 percent of pixels were
below intensity 10 and the two stereo images had as few as 1 and 10 ORB
keypoints. Visual correction was therefore lost before the false flight.

I tested the camera controls directly and found that usb_cam 0.8.1 kept the
device in UVC manual exposure mode even when `autoexposure` was enabled in its
YAML file. I updated the launcher to force UVC automatic exposure mode 3 after
camera startup, with brightness and backlight compensation set to zero. A clean
restart confirmed the requested mode. The launcher now reads the controls back
and stops if the camera rejected automatic mode. Sampled ROS images reached the
1,500 ORB keypoint test cap in each stereo half and had negligible clipping.

I also replayed the earlier failure with a 10 ms camera-to-IMU offset. It
worsened the maximum displacement to 16.32 m, compared with 0.958 m using the
calibrated 20 ms offset and the current innovation gate. I restored and kept
the 20 ms calibration. A two-minute continuous desk-motion test is still needed
to confirm that the exposure correction prevents false meter-scale flight.

A later live restart showed that the camera could report automatic mode while
remaining nearly black. I measured only 10 to 21 features. Keeping the 5 ms
exposure and raising gain from 120 to 255 restored about 1,400 features and 81
valid stereo matches. I changed the launcher to apply and verify this setting.
The IMU then delivered 16,653 clean samples before its serial output stopped;
the USB device remained connected. I preserved the diagnostic run and will use
a lighter recording for the next motion test.

I completed the lighter continuous-motion test with the verified 5 ms and gain
255 setting. The path crossed the 1.72 m desk bound after about 64 seconds and
reached 7.46 m. The IMU remained near 99 Hz without packet, I2C or saturation
faults, and the camera still showed about 1,480 features after the failure.

I compared the logs with the OpenVINS documentation, source configuration and
reported failures. The matching signature is visual features being rejected by
the MSCKF innovation test, followed by unconstrained IMU propagation. A replay
with online extrinsic and time-offset calibration estimated about 8.4 to 10.5
ms instead of the assumed 20 ms, but still diverged to over 1 km. I therefore
stopped changing gates and exposure. The required correction is a full Kalibr
stereo and camera-IMU calibration using raw images, plus measured Allan-variance
IMU noise values.

I added a protected raw-stereo calibration mode. It records the two lens images
before rectification and refuses to start OpenVINS in that mode. A live check
confirmed 640x480 images at about 30 Hz with the real lens distortion model,
while the IMU stayed near 99 Hz without gaps, corruption or saturation. I also
started building the official Kalibr ROS 1 Docker environment.
