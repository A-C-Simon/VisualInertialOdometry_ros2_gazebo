HW290 OpenVINS drift investigation

Work completed

I traced the 39.90 m failure to dark frames and visual-update loss. Automatic
exposure was unreliable, so I set and verified 5 ms exposure with gain 255.
This restored about 1,400 features and 81 stereo matches.

I ran a lighter continuous desk test. The IMU stayed near 99 Hz without packet,
I2C or saturation faults, but the path crossed the 1.72 m desk bound after
about 64 seconds and reached 7.46 m. About 1,480 features remained visible.
Another run logged 44.75 m with clean IMU delivery; I did not measure its motion.

Finding

The logs match reported OpenVINS failures where MSCKF rejects visual updates
and IMU propagation then drives the position away. Online extrinsic and timing
calibration estimated an 8.4 to 10.5 ms offset but still diverged. The current
photo-estimated transform, assumed timing and guessed IMU noise need measurement;
the exact cause of visual rejection is still unconfirmed.

Next step

recording mode. ROS bag conversion passed on 8,870 IMU messages. My screen
target measured about 40 mm tags with 12 mm gaps. I built the C++ Allan tool
and checked its curves on the short IMU bag; the three-hour measurement is pending.

I built a C++ screen recorder with stereo previews, red and green cues and a
countdown. I fixed the 20 Hz preview rate error and camera-resolution tag
detection. A separate test verified the green cue and saved bag; I checked the
live camera preview. Physical calibration and the final desk test remain pending.
